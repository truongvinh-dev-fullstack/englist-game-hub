# Content Guidelines

## Initial scope
300 common words, 12 grammar topics, 20 scripted workplace/daily-life conversations. Topic mix: Office & Meetings, Email & Scheduling, Phone Calls, Customer Service, Interview, Travel, Food & Socializing. Levels A0–B1 (internal tags; do not claim official certification).

## Editorial rules
Natural English, clear Vietnamese translation, appropriate workplace register, one learning objective per question, explanation 1–3 sentences, plausible distractors but exactly one correct answer unless accepted variants declared. Prefer concise prompts and inclusive examples. Distinguish British/American variants where relevant.

## Workflow
Draft -> Linguistic Review -> QA in game -> Published -> Archived. Required fields: topic, skill, difficulty, correct solution, explanation, source/licensing for audio/images, reviewer, version. Import CSV in draft state only; publish after validation.

## Content seed example
`I usually check my emails in the morning.` — concept: present simple + frequency adverb; accepted token order must be explicit. For Roleplay: 'Could we reschedule our meeting?' appropriate, 'Move it now.' inappropriate in a polite business context.

## Media
Only own, licensed or permitted assets. Audio can be generated using an authorized TTS provider after quality check; keep transcripts private before answer when required. Never assume free-to-use images from web search.
